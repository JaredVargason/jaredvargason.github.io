A music app for Android where songs are graphs: notes joined by arrows, with playheads that wander along them, so a song can play differently every time.

  • Draw notes and connect them; where a note branches, the playhead picks a way, weighted however you like.
  • Several instruments at once, each with its own synth sound, or a drum kit.
  • Make it all for free. Bramble Pro, a one-time purchase, adds audio export, MIDI file export, and MIDI out to synths, computers, and other apps.
